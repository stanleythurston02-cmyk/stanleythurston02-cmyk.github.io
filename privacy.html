Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Alfred is a private, self-hosted assistant used by a single person: its owner. It has no other users, no accounts, and no sign-up. This policy describes what it does with that one owner's own data.

What Alfred accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Alfred accesses the owner's own Google account for four things: reading calendar events, reading mail, reading and adding tasks, and creating draft emails. Alfred cannot send email — it has no permission to do so, and a draft is only ever created for the owner to review and send themselves.

How it is used

Data is used solely to answer the owner's own questions and to assemble a daily summary for them. It is not used for advertising, profiling, training any model, or any purpose beyond answering the person who asked.

Where it is stored

Alfred runs on hardware in the owner's home. Data retrieved from Google is held in memory for the duration of a request and is not retained afterwards. Authorisation credentials are stored on that same hardware, readable only by the service account that uses them. Nothing is stored on third-party servers.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third parties, no analytics, no trackers, and no transfer of data to any other service.

Retention and deletion

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any time from the owner's own Google account security settings, which immediately and permanently ends Alfred's access. Stored credentials are deleted when access is withdrawn.

Contact

This is a personal project with a single user, who is also its author and its only point of contact.
