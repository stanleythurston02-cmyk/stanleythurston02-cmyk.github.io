Alfred

Alfred is a private, self-hosted household assistant. It runs entirely on hardware in one home and is used by one person. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

Alfred can, at its owner's request, read that owner's own calendar, mail and task list in order to answer questions about them and to assemble a morning summary. It does this only with the owner's own Google account and only with permissions the owner has granted.
